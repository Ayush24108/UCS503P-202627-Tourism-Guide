Week 1 – Project Planning

Project: Tourism Guide Planner

Work Done:
During the first week, our team finalized the idea for the Tourism Guide Planner. We discussed the problems faced by tourists while planning trips, such as finding suitable destinations, organizing itineraries, and getting relevant travel information. Based on these discussions, we finalized the project scope and prepared the initial project proposal.

Learning/Outcome:
We gained a clear understanding of the project's objectives, proposed features, target users, and overall system requirements.

Week 2 – Understanding LLMs and AI Chatbot

Work Done:
During the second week, I explored the concept of Large Language Models (LLMs) and studied how they can be used in real-world applications. Based on this learning, I identified that an LLM-powered chatbot could be integrated into our Tourism Guide Planner to provide users with conversational assistance.

The chatbot could help tourists ask questions about destinations, attractions, travel plans, and other tourism-related information in natural language. I also started understanding how an LLM could be connected to our application through an API instead of developing a language model from scratch.

Learning/Outcome:
I developed a basic understanding of LLMs, their role in AI applications, and their potential use in our project. This helped us refine the project's AI component and plan for the implementation of an AI-based tourism chatbot in the upcoming weeks.
