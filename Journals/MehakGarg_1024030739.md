### Work Done
- Configured and successfully ran the **FastAPI-based AI service**.
- Installed the required Python dependencies for the AI service, including `python-dotenv` and the **Google GenAI SDK**.
- Integrated the **Google Gemini API** as the LLM for generating tourism-related responses.
- Tested the AI service using the `/chat` endpoint and verified successful AI-generated responses.

- Implemented personalized trip planning using:

- Sent the trip details from the Node.js backend to the FastAPI AI service and then to Gemini for itinerary generation.
- Added error handling for trip retrieval and AI service communication.

### Outcome
- Successfully integrated **Google Gemini** with the Tourist Guide application.
- Established communication between the **Node.js backend, FastAPI AI service, and Gemini LLM**.
- Implemented personalized AI-based trip planning using user-specific trip information stored in MongoDB.
- The backend can now retrieve a user's trip details and generate a customized day-by-day itinerary based on their destination, duration, budget, and interests.
